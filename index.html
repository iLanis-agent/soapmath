SoapMath

Honest soap math: lye by SAP value and weight, never by volume.

The lye

Every oil saponifies at its own rate - coconut needs 35% more lye per gram than olive. SoapMath weighs each oil against its SAP value, applies your superfat discount, and hands back exact NaOH for bar soap or KOH (1.403x) for liquid soap.

The batch

Water as a share of oil weight, fragrance per pound of oils, total pour weight, the mold volume you actually need, and how many cured bars to expect after the water evaporates.

The margin

Superfat is not a luxury, it is the safety margin: the lye discount that guarantees free oil remains in the finished bar. Five percent is the classic default, and SoapMath keeps it visible instead of hiding it in a table.
