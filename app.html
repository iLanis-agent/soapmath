The oils

Weigh oils on a scale, never in cups - a cup of coconut oil and a cup of olive oil are different amounts of oil, and the lye does not care what your measuring cup thought.

The honest part

Every oil saponifies at its own rate - coconut needs 35% more lye per gram than olive - so "a spoon of lye per cup of oil" is how chemical burns happen. Superfat is the safety margin: the lye discount that leaves free oil in the bar. And lye always goes INTO the water, slowly, never the other way around - the other way around is a volcano.
